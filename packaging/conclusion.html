Ready to set up your labels

  1. Connect and turn on your XP-330B.
  2. Open Open Xprinter in Applications and choose your language in the top-right menu.
  3. Select your loaded label size, stock type and gap. Click Set up printer if needed, then Save as default for other apps.
  4. Print one label to check alignment before printing a batch.

In other apps select Xprinter XP-330B Labels (Open Source) and the matching paper size.

Help and source: github.com/ismoil-nosr/xprinter-macos
