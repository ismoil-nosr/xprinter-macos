Open Xprinter

An open source XP-330B USB label driver and label printing app for macOS 14 or newer, on Apple Silicon and Intel.

Install, connect the printer, then open Open Xprinter in Applications. Select the size of your loaded labels and save it as the printer default.

The initial size is 58 × 40 mm, with a 2 mm gap. The app also supports other label sizes, barcodes, QR codes, PDF, images and CSV.

This installer needs administrator access to install the local print driver. Internet access is not required. Existing printer queues are preserved.
